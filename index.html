How the calculation works

Monthly revenue = daily sales × average sale × operating days × machines. Yearly revenue = monthly revenue × 12, with no seasonality or ramp-up adjustment. Percentage costs apply to total revenue. Other monthly costs are the total for all machines and are subtracted once.

Before you buy

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Make sure the equipment, location, and operating plan fit together.

How much does a machine cost?

Request a current quote for your machine configuration, quantity, and delivery location. Ask for an itemized total so you can see exactly what is included and what costs are additional.

Can I customize the machine’s wrap?

Yes. The manufacturer can apply a custom wrap. Choose the signature blue Frost Bot design or discuss your own brand direction. The Flex Freeze and Gainz Cream protein designs shown are concepts. Confirm final artwork, pricing, and lead time in your quote.

Can I offer protein ice cream?

Protein ice cream is an option to discuss with our team. Confirm the intended recipe, mix compatibility, serving size, and menu with the equipment configuration before purchase. Nutrition varies by recipe and toppings.

What does operating the machine involve?

The customer order and dispensing process is automated. Owners still need to arrange restocking, cleaning, sanitation, routine checks, maintenance, and customer issue handling.

What utilities and space will I need?

The exact requirements depend on the model. Request its specification sheet and check power, connectivity, ventilation clearance, delivery access, and service access with your venue before installation.

Are delivery, installation, and training included?

Confirm these items in your written quote. The scope should specify shipping or delivery, setup, operator training, and any travel or site preparation charges.

Can I explore financing?

Indicate your interest in the optional form details. Ask the team about currently available options and terms. Approval, rates, and required down payments must be confirmed with the financing provider.

How much could I earn?

Results depend on demand, pricing, location costs, operating costs, and uptime. The calculator is a planning tool with hypothetical inputs, not a forecast or guarantee. Validate your assumptions before making a purchase.

What warranty and support come with it?

Request the current written warranty and support terms with your quote, including coverage, exclusions, parts, labor, service availability, and response procedures.
